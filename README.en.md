## Profile

I am a cybersecurity professional with experience in pentesting, technical assessment, and security consulting.

My work focuses on assessing the real exposure of systems, applications, and APIs by combining offensive testing, technical analysis, and consulting judgment to identify weaknesses, validate impact, and define remediation measures that are useful for both business and technical teams.

My role is not limited to finding vulnerabilities. It is about answering four key questions:

- Is there a real and exploitable weakness?
- What is the operational or business impact?
- What technical evidence supports it?
- What should be fixed, in what order, and why?

## Working approach

### Understand the context
Before running tools, I analyze scope, exposed surface, criticality, and test objectives.

### Enumerate with intent
Enumeration is not about collecting noise. It is about reducing uncertainty.

### Validate manually
Automation accelerates the process. Human analysis confirms exploitation, impact, and relevance.

### Prioritize real risk
Not every finding matters equally. I prioritize exposure, abuse potential, and consequences.

### Report for decision-making
A good report does not just describe the flaw. It enables remediation.
